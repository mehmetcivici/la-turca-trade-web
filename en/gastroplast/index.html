Gastroplast is the main brand of SürPlast Plastik, an Istanbul manufacturer founded in 2015 that exports to more than 55 countries. It supplies hotels, restaurants, caterers and the food industry.

The company designs and builds its own moulds. Making the mould and the part in the same plant lowers cost, tightens quality control and shortens lead times for new models.

Its PC and PP gastronorm pans and lids are listed by NSF International. The polycarbonate it uses has been food-contact tested to FDA and EU requirements. Gastroplast has a branch and warehouse in Budapest, Hungary, and exhibits at fairs such as Ambiente (Frankfurt) and Host (Milan).

SürPlast brands: GastroPlast, GastroCups and GastroCarry.

Guide for importers

Why buy vacuum flasks from Türkiye

  • Factory price and traceability

    Star Termos makes its jugs and flasks in Istanbul and Gastroplast designs and builds its own moulds. You buy at plant price and know where every piece was made.

  • Certified quality

    Star Termos holds ISO 9001, 14001, 22000 and 45001 and is a Sedex member. Gastroplast GN pans are listed by NSF International.

  • Flexible orders

    Mixed containers from both factories, colours per item and manufacturing under your brand (OEM and ODM).

  • Logistics from Istanbul

    Both plants are in Esenyurt, about 20 km from Ambarlı port, where we ship to the Atlantic and Pacific coasts.

How to import from Türkiye, step by step

  1. 1

    Range and quantities

    Pick items from the catalogs and check the volume with the container calculator. You can combine products from both factories.

  2. 2

    Quote and samples

    We send prices in USD (FOB Istanbul, CFR or CIF to your port) and samples of the chosen items.

  3. 3

    Proforma and deposit

    The proforma confirms units per carton, volume, weight and production time. The factory starts on the agreed deposit.

  4. 4

    Production and inspection

    We follow production and check quantities, packing and marking before loading. You receive photos and video.

  5. 5

    Shipping and documents

    We send the commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and bill of lading.

  6. 6

    Clearance at destination

    Your customs broker clears the goods with those documents; we supply any technical information needed.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Why buy vacuum flasks from Turkish manufacturers?

For the mix of certified quality, factory prices and flexibility: mixed containers, colours per item and private label. Star Termos has 30 years in the industry and exports to 19 countries.

Which Turkish thermos manufacturer do you represent?

We represent Star Termos, an Istanbul maker of double-glass thermal jugs, steel flasks, tumblers and food jars. For food service equipment we represent Gastroplast.

What is the minimum order?

It depends on the item. For a first order we suggest a mixed 20' container; the calculator shows how many units fit.

How long does an order from Türkiye to South America take?

Production time plus sea transit, which depends on the carrier and transshipments. Both are stated on the proforma.

Are Turkish vacuum flasks certified?

The Star Termos plant is certified to ISO 9001, ISO 14001, ISO 22000 (food safety) and ISO 45001 and is a Sedex member. Products carry a 2-year manufacturing warranty.

Can I put my brand on the flasks?

Yes. Star Termos manufactures OEM and ODM: logo on the product, custom colours and a gift box with your artwork and barcode.

How is payment handled?

Usually bank transfer with deposit and balance against documents, or letter of credit. Agreed per order.

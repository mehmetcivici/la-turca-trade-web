OEM · ODM

Private label

If your company already has a recognised brand in your country, we can manufacture under it. Star Termos produces OEM and ODM for other brands; we coordinate design, samples and production.

Request a quote

YOUR BRAND

What can be customised

  • Logo on the product

    Laser engraving on stainless steel, screen or pad printing on plastic surfaces, depending on the model.

  • Colours

    Body, lid and details in your brand colours, subject to minimums per colour.

  • Box and packing

    Gift box with your artwork, Spanish or Portuguese copy and your EAN barcode. Master carton with your marking.

  • Manuals and labels

    User manual and labelling according to your country's regulations.

How it works

  1. 1

    Brief

    Send us your vector logo, the models you are interested in and estimated quantities.

  2. 2

    Proposal

    We prepare product and packaging visuals with price and minimums per item.

  3. 3

    Sample

    We make a physical sample for your approval before production.

  4. 4

    Production and control

    Mass production with inspection of marking, colour and packing before loading.

Private label minimums are higher than for standard products and are confirmed per item.

For Gastroplast items, customisation is assessed case by case, depending on the part and the volume.

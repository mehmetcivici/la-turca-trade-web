From first contact to container

  1. 1

    Enquiry

    Tell us what you distribute, in which country and to which port. We send catalogs and the price list.

  2. 2

    Samples

    We shortlist items and send samples so you can validate quality and price in your market.

  3. 3

    Proforma

    We plan the full or mixed container with units per carton, volume and weight.

  4. 4

    Production

    Once the deposit is in, the factory produces. We report progress and any change.

  5. 5

    Inspection and loading

    We check the goods in Istanbul. You get photos and video before the container is sealed.

  6. 6

    Shipping and documents

    We send the bill of lading and document set and track the shipment to your port.

FOB · CFR · CIF

Commercial terms

Currency
Prices in US dollars.
Incoterms
FOB Istanbul, CFR or CIF to the destination port.
Payment
Bank transfer with deposit and balance against documents, or letter of credit. Agreed per order.
Minimums
Depend on the item. For a first order we suggest a mixed 20' container.
Documents
Proforma, commercial invoice, packing list, certificate of origin and B/L. Other certificates on request.
Languages
Spanish, Portuguese, English and Turkish.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Can I combine Star Termos and Gastroplast products in one container?

Yes. Both factories are in Esenyurt, Istanbul, and we consolidate the cargo before it goes to Ambarlı port.

Do you sell small quantities?

We work with importers and distributors. For a first order we suggest a mixed 20' container; exact minimums depend on each item.

Do you issue a certificate of origin?

Yes. The products are made in Türkiye and a certificate of origin is issued with every shipment.

How long does an order take?

It depends on the items, factory stock and the carrier. Production time and estimated sailing date are stated on the proforma.

Can you send samples?

Yes, we send samples of the selected items before the first order.

Tell us what you distribute and where your port is

We reply with a price list, catalogs and a proposal for a first order.

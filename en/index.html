Star Termos · Gastroplast

Two factories in Esenyurt, 20 km from the port

Star Termos and Gastroplast both manufacture in Esenyurt, on the European side of Istanbul, about 20 km from Ambarlı port. Being this close lets us consolidate both factories into one container and supervise loading in person.

Star Termos

Vacuum jugs and flasks from 250 ml to 8 litres: double-glass refills, 304 stainless steel, travel tumblers and food jars. A family business founded by Mehmet Dinçok.

  • Exports to 19 countries
  • ISO 9001, 14001, 22000 and 45001
  • Sedex member
  • OEM and ODM manufacturing
About Star Termos

Gastroplast

Equipment for hotels, restaurants and catering in polycarbonate, polypropylene, Tritan and ABS: gastronorm pans, storage containers, dispensers, drinkware and trays. Designs and builds its own moulds.

  • Exports to 55+ countries
  • NSF-listed GN pans
  • FDA and EU food-contact testing
  • Branch and warehouse in Budapest

How we work with distributors

  • Factory prices in USD

    FOB Istanbul, CFR or CIF to your port. No middlemen between you and the plant.

    We speak Spanish, Portuguese, English and Turkish. You deal with one person, not two factories.

    We report sailing date, transshipments and estimated arrival at your port.
